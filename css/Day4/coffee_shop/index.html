ABOUT THE COFFEESHOP COMPANY

Coffeeshop Company is an Austrian coffeehouse chain headquartered in Vienna, recognized internationally by its signature slogan "Home in Vienna". Founded in 1999 as part of the Schärf group of companies, it combines traditional Viennese coffeehouse culture with modern espresso bar concepts.

  • Origin & Heritage: Rooted in Viennese tradition, focusing on high-quality Arabica coffee beans roasted in-house using specialized techniques.
  • Global Presence: Operates around 100 locations across multiple countries in Europe, Asia, and the Middle East through a franchise network.
  • Core Concept: Built around offering a personal "ME Moment" — a warm, urban space designed for relaxation and enjoyment amidst busy city life.
  • Product Line: Offers traditional Viennese specialties, espresso-based beverages, tea, handcrafted cold drinks, snacks, and desserts.
