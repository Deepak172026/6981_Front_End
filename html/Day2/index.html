COLLEGE LIFE

Summar Days:

I finished my 12th public exam, and I was very happy because my exams were finally over. I could spend my summer holidays with my friends without worrying about studies or college. I enjoyed those days a lot. One day, my grandfather told me about a relative named Sekar. He said that Sekar knew about good colleges and asked me to talk to him. I called Sekar and asked him about colleges. He suggested a college in another city and told me that I could stay in a hostel. I had never stayed in a hostel before, so I did not know what hostel life would be like. Still, I decided to give it a try.

Choice Selection:

I also had two choices. I could join a college in my hometown or go to another city. I was confused about which one to choose. One day, I had an argument with my grandmother, and after that, I decided to go to another city and study there. I thought it would be a good chance to experience a new place.

Going to Hostel:

After the holidays, I packed my things and went to the railway station with my grandfather. My friends also came to the station to see me off. Unfortunately, I missed my train. I thought another train would come soon, but there was no suitable train after that. So, I decided to take a bus and finally reached the hostel.

Going to Hostel:

When I arrived at the hostel, I did not like it. I did not know anyone, and I felt lonely. I even thought about changing my college. One day, I went home and told my friend about it. We went to another college to ask about admission, but unfortunately, the admission was already closed. So, I decided to continue in the same college.

Hostel Life:

We went to another college to ask about admission, but unfortunately, the admission was already closed. So, I decided to continue in the same college.

College life Started:

The first few months were not easy. I missed my hometown, my family, and my old friends. Slowly, things started to change. I made friends with Mahesh, Lokesh, Sam, Keshav, Ragu, and Joy. In the hostel, I became friends with Ayyanar. I also had a senior named Madhesh who was very close to me and treated me like a brother. After some time, I started enjoying both college and hostel life. The second year was much more fun. We played games in class, skipped classes, went outside for tea, ate biryani, and sometimes came back late to class. We also started trying to talk to girls. One day, Sam and I went to the Science Block looking for a girl. A mam caught us, but somehow we escaped and went to another block. It was a funny moment that we still remember. After the second year, we entered our final year. We were happy to be together, but we also felt a little sad because we knew college would soon be over. We started taking more photos and enjoying our time together.

College life Ending:

Finally, I completed my college degree. I was very happy, but when I looked at my friends, I also felt emotional. We had started as strangers, but slowly they became an important part of my life. I left college with many memories in my heart. I learned one important thing: sometimes, the place we dislike at first can become the place we never want to leave.
